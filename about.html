About Us

The Story Behind Our Cinnamon

Welcome to AlBA Garde Ceylon, where tradition, quality, and the natural richness of Sri Lanka come together.

Our journey begins in the heart of Ceylon, where cinnamon has been cultivated and carefully prepared for generations. We believe that true quality comes from respecting the land, the people, and the traditional methods that make Ceylon cinnamon unique.

From the delicate harvesting of the cinnamon bark to the careful preparation of each quill, every step matters. Our goal is to bring the authentic character of Ceylon cinnamon to customers who appreciate natural products and exceptional quality.

Our Heritage

Ceylon cinnamon has a long history deeply connected to Sri Lanka. Known for its delicate aroma, warm flavour, and naturally sweet character, it has become one of the island's most recognised natural products.

At Tropical Spice Garden, we are inspired by this heritage and work to preserve the traditional character of Ceylon cinnamon while presenting it for the modern world.

Our Commitment

Quality

We believe quality should never be compromised. We carefully select our cinnamon to ensure that our products meet our standards.

Tradition

We respect the traditional knowledge and craftsmanship behind Ceylon cinnamon and aim to preserve these practices.

Authenticity

Our focus is on authentic Ceylon cinnamon and its distinctive natural characteristics.

Bringing the authentic taste and heritage of Ceylon cinnamon to your table.
